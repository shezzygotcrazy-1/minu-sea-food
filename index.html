From water to warehouse

Six handovers, each with a temperature.

Every lot passes the same checkpoints. Targets shown are typical for frozen product; fresh-chilled lots stay on ice.

  1. 0 to 4°C

    Landing

    Catch is received, iced and logged by source and time.

  2. 0 to 4°C

    Grading

    Sorted by species, count per kg and quality.

  3. Below 10°C

    Processing

    Washed, cleaned and cut to the order form.

  4. -35°C or lower

    Freezing

    IQF or block, frozen fast to hold texture.

  5. -18°C or lower

    Packing

    Glazed, weighed, boxed and labelled with batch codes.

  6. -18°C or lower

    Dispatch

    Loaded into reefers with temperature records.

How do you keep product fresh?

Product is iced at landing, processed in a controlled area, frozen quickly and held at storage temperature until a reefer collects it. Temperature is recorded at each step.

Yes. Tell us the species, size grade, form (whole, fillet, peeled, IQF or block), pack weight and labelling, and we will confirm what is possible.
